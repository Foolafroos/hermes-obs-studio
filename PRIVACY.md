# Privacy Policy — Hermes OBS Studio

**Last updated:** May 2026

## Overview
Hermes OBS Studio is a WebSocket controller for OBS Studio, enabling AI-driven scene management. Runs locally.

## Data Handling
- Communicates with OBS Studio via local WebSocket connection
- No data is transmitted to external services
- No telemetry or analytics

## Your Data
- OBS scene configurations stay on your machine
- No persistent data storage beyond OBS state

## Third-Party Services
- None. Local-only operation.
